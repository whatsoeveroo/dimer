How it works

What is inside

One small computer, the ESP32-S3, runs everything. It plugs into the custom board. The motor driver, speaker amplifier, microphone and 5 V converter are chips on that board, fitted at the factory. Everything else plugs into a labelled connector or clamps into a screw terminal.

  ESP <-- Wi-Fi --> AI[OpenAI API: hear, think, speak]

{{PARTS_HTML}}
  • Chassis tub: holds the motors, battery and main board. Four ears on top for bolting your shell on, a tab at each corner for an edge sensor, and two round holes in the rear wall for the switch and the charging socket.
  • Neck bridge: holds the servo that turns the head.
  • Head box: a 46 × 72 × 62 mm placeholder with a window for the display and a smaller one for the distance sensor. Replace it with your own head.
  • Shovel: two arms and a blade, lifted by one servo on the left wall.

The bought parts are drawn from typical seller dimensions, and the main board is a simplified model until the real one is routed. Track and wheel sizes are placeholders. Measure the parts that arrive before final fits.
